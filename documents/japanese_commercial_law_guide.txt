JAPAN COMPREHENSIVE COMMERCIAL AND STATUTORY REGULATORY REFORM CODE OF 2026 ================================================================================ ACT NO. 402 OF 2026 (10,000 WORD EDITION) Jurisdiction: National Diet of Japan Classification: Codified Commercial Law & Statutory Framework Benchmark ================================================================================ ================================================================================ TITLE I: GENERAL PROVISIONS AND STATUTORY INTERPRETATION ================================================================================ ------------------------------------------------------------ CHAPTER 1: Fundamental Principles and Scope ------------------------------------------------------------ Article 1 (Statutory Governance and Regulatory Mandates Part 1) 1. In respect of commercial transactions governed by this Code and pursuant to the Financial Services Agency (FSA), every commercial merchant, entity, statutory representative, or obligor shall exercise the standard of care of a prudent manager (zenbun-kanyūsha no chūi-gimu). Where an obligor fails to observe the standards prescribed in Paragraph 1 of Article 1, the injured counterparty or registered shareholder shall be entitled to claim full compensatory damages or injunctive relief before the competent District Court having jurisdiction over the registered principal office. 2. In respect of commercial transactions governed by this Code and pursuant to the Commercial Code (Act No. 48 of 1899), every commercial merchant, entity, statutory representative, or obligor shall exercise the standard of care of a prudent manager (zenbun-kanyūsha no chūi-gimu). Where an obligor fails to observe the standards prescribed in Paragraph 2 of Article 1, the injured counterparty or registered shareholder shall be entitled to claim full compensatory damages or injunctive relief before the competent District Court having jurisdiction over the registered principal office. (1) Sub-item A: Statutory filings required under this paragraph must be submitted within thirty (30) business days. (2) Sub-item B: Non-compliance shall incur an administrative fine (*過料, karyō*) not exceeding Five Million Yen (¥5,000,000). 3.
